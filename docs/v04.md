# Sweetybot2 v04: stronger actuation and sampled sensors

This revision updates the root CAD, BOM, MJCF/URDF, native servo model and learning objective together. Earlier v02 training artifacts remain historical and incompatible with the new 591-observation actor. No physical robot or motor purchase is implied.

## Mechanical build

8 SM105BL pitch/knee + 4 SM80BL roll + 2 STS3250 neck + 2 STS3032 ears. Body 240×112×88mm, 95/100mm leg links, 164mm foot-centre track. Hip roll is limited to ±0.4rad after the larger knee envelopes interfered at the old ±0.55rad extremes. Offset rear shin supports return to the hoof plane; structural clearance primitives are included in training. Mass comes from placed CAD BRep volumes and tensors: 6061 load parts, solid PETG cosmetics and TPU pads, manufacturer motor masses, explicit unweighed electronics/fastener/wire allowances. Hardware internals use uniform envelopes. Print-envelope check is 256mm; structural parts require metal fabrication. Saved CAD and simulation share configuration and link frames.

Static BRep motor/structural intersections and motor/electronics box sweeps are retained as reports; joint-range warnings remain an assembly gate. A home-pose clearance is not a full motion/cable/tolerance certificate. Horns, passive bearings, screws and housing rigidity require physical fit and load testing.

## Sensor contract

Torso LSM6DSV16X is rigidly mounted at [10,0,29]mm; axes +X forward, +Y left, +Z up. MuJoCo gyro and accelerometer are sampled into a 120Hz noisy, biased orientation estimator. Accelerometer measures specific force: approximately +g upward at rest, zero in freefall. No root linear velocity, global position, absolute yaw, true foot forces or terrain truth enters the actor. Reset orientation is a noisy fused-IMU boot prior, modelling an estimator running before a fall; it is not an independently demonstrated cold-start solution.

Four Omron switch hooves are binary, with 200Hz sampling, 10ms debounce, bounce/noise and fault masks. The 0.8mm floating-sole assembly has a hard stop carrying the load; simulation uses a rigid tread with compliant MuJoCo contact and an effective ~2.36N axial-load switch threshold. It does not explicitly simulate sole travel, spring deflection or the microswitch internals. Contact force is privileged reward/evaluation data only. EOL resistor loops distinguish air/contact/open/short; stale or faulty samples are invalid. This threshold must be measured on one real cassette.

Chest VL53L5CX: 8×8 ray ranges, 15Hz, 65° diagonal, 20° downward, opening at torso [135,0,-10]mm. ToF includes missing ranges, limited 0.03..4m range, noise and stale masks. Rays approximate geometry and do not reproduce material multipath or device confidence. Camera Module 3 Wide is at head [78,0,67]mm; pinhole vertical FOV 67° with calibration pending. RGB is available for rendering/perception but is not used by this actor.

One frame is 197 values; three history frames give 591. Exact named slices and deployable encoder are in hardware/observation.py. Normalised gravity/gyro, 16 encoder offsets/velocities, previous 12 actions, command/phase, accelerometer, four binary contacts + validity, IMU validity/age, 64 ranges + validity and depth age. Old 165-input checkpoints are rejected. Replay switching uses sampled IMU tilt and support-foot kinematic height rather than simulator root pose; height is held during flight and IMU staleness stops replay. hardware/telemetry.py implements offline CRC32/sequence/timestamp validation and contact decoding. MCU firmware, serial I/O, ToF/camera drivers, timestamp synchronisation and physical deployment remain unverified.

## Rewards and evaluation

Walk/run: command and yaw tracking, posture, height, action smoothness, penalties for supporting-foot slip, body collisions, mechanical work and motor overload. Running adds one reward at completion of a clean >=40ms forward flight interval; jumping in place, mismatching commands and hanging airborne do not qualify. The independent running gate still requires repeated forward flights, measured speed, survival and each command/surface.

Recovery: discounted potential progress in orientation/height/posture, elapsed-time cost, stable support and one completion event. Stable recovery requires >=3 supporting feet, no body contact, low horizontal speed, upright torso and nominal-height bounds for 0.7s. Reset mixture favours left/right/supine falls, with a difficulty curriculum and full falls from the beginning. `folded_crouch` is an upright folded pose; the old `prone` alias never represented a belly-down fall.

New held-out final seeds are 4000000..4000099. Final evaluation is separate from development and preview. A CPU PPO smoke verifies optimiser/checkpoint/contract plumbing only; there is no trained v04 policy or any-terrain success claim in this build. The already approved v02 training run is kept separate; a new GPU budget is not silently charged.

## Rebuild

CAD Python 3.13: `python -m scripts.build_cad`, `python -m scripts.check_cad`, `python -m scripts.reconcile_mass`, `python -m scripts.export_link_meshes`. Use `CADGEN_DAEMON=0` for cold dynamic-factory builds. Learning Python 3.11/3.12: install learning/requirements.txt; `python -m scripts.generate_descriptions`, `python -m learning.build_native`, `python -m pytest -q`, `python -m scripts.validate_v04`. `python -m scripts.feasibility` and `python -m scripts.engineering_package` regenerate motor and purchasing reports. Rerun CAD assembly after geometry changes. Do not load v02 weights into v04.
