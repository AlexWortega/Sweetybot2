# Sweetybot2 v05: mechanical redesign and matching RL

This is a digital prototype. V04 was withdrawn after exact CAD interference checks failed. Its release remains withdrawn. V05 uses independently rotating front/rear plates, bearing carriers, accessible M3 flange heads and a matching CAD-derived collision model. Physical strength, purchased interfaces and trained capability are separate acceptance stages.

The robot is inspired by [Sweetie Bot](https://github.com/sweetie-bot-project/sweetie_bot) and [Microduck](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck). It is an independent quadruped design; upstream controllers and hardware are not claimed compatible.

## Assembly

- 12 SM105BL-C001 leg motors, two STS3250 neck motors and two STS3032 ears. Structural parts are machined 6061 aluminium; PETG cosmetic covers and hoof carriers; TPU tread.
- Body 240×136×88mm; foot-centre track 200mm; thigh/shin 115/110mm. Nominal foot-centre stance height 275mm, root height with tread 296mm. The higher stance reduces static knee moment; it does not establish a running speed.
- Hind pitch/knee axes and their actual hardware are reflected fore-aft, so rear legs fold rearward. Front/hind and left/right custom instances are exported in `cad/manufacturing/`; four identical generic foot/motor carriers are not a valid build.
- Hip roll ±0.15rad, hip pitch −1.5..0.1rad, knee 0.1..2.1rad. Front/rear legs interfere at some combinations outside the operating cohort even within individual joint limits. The Cartesian product is not certified; contacts remain active in simulation and invalid reset poses are rejected.
- Eight 6807 bearing envelopes (35×47×7mm), bolted rear rotor sleeves and retainers support the hip/knee interfaces. Eight M3 holes on 26mm PCD connect each SM105 output flange. There are 160 explicit ≤6mm-diameter, ≤3mm-high M3 head envelopes on those flanges, with tool access through the roll carrier.
- Motor cases seat on 0.75/0.80mm rigid shim stock in external clamps; axial stops retain SM105 cases. Two long M3 tie bolts attach each hip stator cradle to its bearing carrier; knee cradles tie into both thigh cheeks with gauged spacers. Check actual bolt lengths, nut access and engagement on the purchased hardware. CAD fastener heads do not substitute for a fastener strength calculation.
- The upstream STS3250 STEP contains a split surface assembly that produced invalid compound boolean intersections. The physical keepout uses its closed enclosing case; the upstream file remains a reference. Neck/ear spline/horn interfaces still require actual parts and fit coupons.

Mass and the full per-link inertia tensors are computed from placed CAD volumes and catalogue/allowance masses in [mass_budget.json](../reports/mass_budget.json). This is not a scale measurement. [Actuator screen](feasibility.md) includes 20% mass contingency, a worst loaded leg taking 50% weight, 1.5 torque margin, 9.3V cutoff and 0.8rad/s. Catalogue rated points and the I²t surrogate are not measured thermal endurance. The main fuse/BMS must handle real transients; 12 stalled leg motors alone have a 75.6A catalogue sum, above the 40A main fuse.

## Geometry acceptance

1. `check_assembly_fits` tests all physical part pairs on each rigid link, using exact solid intersections. Solid pairs are tested separately: OCC compounds sharing a face can otherwise report an intersection outside both solids. Display pigments are excluded; purchased groups of bolts/shims are explicit groups, not single printable parts.
2. `find_motion_candidates` checks the full assembly's triangle surfaces across 140 operational poses: home, seven samples per joint, 25 coordinated gait phases and two recovery crouches. A further 100 uniform random configurations are diagnostics, not a certified operating envelope.
3. `check_motion_candidates` confirms triangle candidates with exact BRep solid intersections. Tangency and mesh faceting are not automatically treated as interference. The gate is bound to source hashes.
4. `validate_v05` checks the corresponding MuJoCo collision model through the same operational poses. Passing CAD while convex proxies invent contacts is a failure. Parent-child collision filtering is disabled. Whole brackets and covers remain self-collidable.

Convex decomposition uses [CoACD](https://github.com/SarahWeiii/CoACD). Thresholds are nominal, not certified distance bounds; a hull cap can exceed them. Head/cowl interfaces therefore have uncapped decomposition and explicit operational-pose checks. Head pieces additionally have a conservative 0.9mm cube Minkowski padding; sampled real surfaces must fall within the reported 1mm proxy tolerance (0.3mm coverage is also recorded). All articulated poses are rechecked after padding. Tiny fastener bores and selected sealed cavities are filled conservatively. Bought servo cases, external clamp pieces and ears have explicit convex envelopes. Couplers, bearing rolling interfaces and soft mane/tail are world-collidable but excluded from self-contact constraints; their internal fits are checked in CAD. Tread and floating sole share one contact box so their floor forces are not double-counted.

These are sampled digital checks, not continuous collision detection, tolerance certification, fatigue testing or approval to fabricate an unattended robot.

## Sensors and learning

Torso LSM6DSV16X at [10,0,29]mm, +X forward/+Y left/+Z up. Native MuJoCo gyro and specific-force accelerometer sample at 500Hz; a biased/noisy estimator runs at 120Hz. Freefall produces approximately zero specific force. A noisy reset boot prior models an already running estimator; it is not a tested cold-start solution.

Four binary hoof switches sample at 200Hz with 10ms debounce, bounce/fault masks and resistor-coded ADC loop health. Load bypasses the microswitch through the carrier, sole guides and hard stop. Simulation uses a rigid sole contact with an effective force threshold, not an explicit moving spring/switch mechanism. Real cassette stroke and force require calibration. Open/short/stale wires are invalid, never confirmed air.

Chest VL53L5CX: 8×8 ranges, 15Hz, 65° diagonal, 20° downward, opening [135,0,−10]mm. Frames are held between measurements, with range/noise/stale masks. Forehead Camera Module 3 Wide at [78,0,91]mm is available for RGB rendering; RGB is not an actor input. Optical calibration, material multipath and real device drivers remain physical work.

The sensor transport retains 197 values per frame. The default compact trainer projects one frame to 63 values: estimated gravity/gyro, all 16 joint positions/velocities, 12 previous leg actions, velocity command, contact switches/health and IMU health. ToF, RGB, accelerometer and gait clock are excluded. An 81-value privileged critic receives clean proprioception plus true velocity, height and foot/contact measurements during training only. The legacy 3×197 = 591 actor remains an explicit mode; old weights cannot be loaded as compact weights. See [architecture, distillation and continuation](compact-policy.md). MCU packet CRC/sequence/timestamp decoding and GPIO ownership are tested offline; firmware and serial/device drivers have not been commissioned.

Walk/run rewards track commands and posture, penalise foot slip, work, motor overload and self-collision. Forward flight earns a single event after ≥40ms of clean forward flight; jumping in place does not qualify. Recovery rewards potential progress and a ≥0.7s upright, feet-supported stable stand. It rejects body bracing and self-contact as stable success. `folded_crouch` is an upright folded reset, not a belly-down fall. Replay uses IMU tilt and support-foot kinematics, with walk/run selection, deadman and recovery priority.

Checkpoint compatibility includes a dynamics hash of configuration, model, collision mesh bytes and sensor/reward code, plus explicit actor/critic architecture. V02/v04 policies are incompatible. Training stops before CUDA allocation when mechanical/physics checks fail. Champion promotion requires independent command/orientation/terrain results, measured torque limits, no self-contact and repeated forward flight for running. Valid PPO continuation is independent of promotion; a poor quality score no longer rewinds the optimizer. A local optimizer smoke is not a learned-quality result. The legacy campaign already consumed its final cohort 4000000..4000099; compact revision 3 reserves 5000000..5000099, queried only once at the end of a newly authorized cycle.

## Rebuild

CAD: Python 3.13 with `cadgen==0.7.21`. Learning/native: Python 3.11/3.12 with `learning/requirements.txt`. Collision build: Python 3.11 with `simulation/collision-requirements.txt`. Set `CAD_PY`, `CADGEN`, `LEARNING_PY`, `COLLISION_PY` to your actual interpreters/CLI.

```sh
make setup setup-learning setup-collision
make cad collisions model
make audit test simulate
make render
make package
```

`cad_snapshot.json` proves volume/solid-count round trips for every saved component and caches the exact placed STEP solids and is invalidated by geometry/source changes. Mass-only reconciliation preserves the geometry signature. Do not reuse a running source tree or checkpoint directory from an older revision. Keep the approved historical 24-hour v02 run untouched; a new long training cycle needs its own budget and fresh output directory.
