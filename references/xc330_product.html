    Key Features

    Key Features of DYNAMIXEL XC330 Series

    All-Metal Gear System for Enhanced Durability
    The XC330 series features a full metal gear construction, significantly improving durability and resistance to wear compared to previous models.

    Compact and Lightweight Design
    With dimensions of 20x34x26 mm and weighing just 23g, the XC330 actuators offer a compact form factor suitable for space-constrained applications.

    High-Resolution Feedback
    Equipped with a 4096-step encoder, the XC330 provides precise position feedback, enabling fine-grained control in robotic systems.

    Versatile Communication Protocols
    The actuators support TTL, SBUS, iBUS, and RC PWM communication protocols, offering flexibility in integration with various control systems.

    Multiple Operating Modes
    The XC330 series supports six operating modes: Position Control, Extended Position Control, Velocity Control, Current Control, PWM Control, and Current-Based Position Control, providing versatility for different robotic applications.

    Take full control of your DYNAMIXEL servos with our robust software ecosystem, specifically designed for seamless programming and control. Here's an overview of what's available
    Leverage the power and versatility of DYNAMIXEL actuators with our multi-platform Software Development Kit (SDK).     Discover DYNAMIXEL Wizard, a comprehensive software solution designed to seamlessly manage and configure your DYNAMIXEL servos.     DYNAMIXEL servos are also designed for seamless integration with the Robot Operating System (ROS 2). Leveraging ROS' comprehensive services and DYNAMIXEL's versatility, you can elevate your robotic systems, enabling complex interactions to power highly capable robotic systems.
